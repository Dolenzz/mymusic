# Approved direction and next decisions

## Identity

Tom’s Music Library: warm charcoal, ivory, copper. Album art supplies color. Editorial serif headings with readable sans-serif body text. Professional and personal without visual clutter. PC browsers are primary; mobile and tablets remain fully usable.

## Browsing

Collection, decade, and artist browsing all combine matching album editions by default. Sorting by Format (either direction) or enabling Group by format displays individual editions. Combined grid cards use a subtle stack of two or three covers plus combined format labels; album pages retain edition switching.

On individual decade pages, the personal introduction shares a compact desktop header with the decade title in a subtle box on the right. Phones show a collapsed “My listening in the [decade]s” disclosure. The main Decades page keeps the full stories visible.

Home generates a fresh random selection on every visit, refresh, and shuffle, while retaining all shelf exclusions and the fixed banner albums, avoiding a requirement to maintain editorial content. Collection has three cover sizes and a sortable table. Artist pages display each matching album once with combined format labels; grouping by format shows each owned edition in its section. Permanent IDs and edition-specific metadata remain intact.

Natural artist display names and catalog surname sorting are separate. Optional leading The, punctuation, accents, and name-order aliases should not obstruct search. Spotify buttons appear where album links exist; no invented links or search links posing as matched albums.

Decades currently filter release dates. Personal listening history is different from release chronology. Draft personal decade introductions with Tom before adding them; do not invent his buying or listening history. Favorites may be added gradually later and are not required for the home page to work.

## Artwork reminder

“From the shelves” and the home random-album button exclude Comedy, Spoken Word, Jodeci, and CD/cassette singles (including maxi-singles). Singles compilations and short full albums remain eligible. These exclusions do not remove anything from collection, artist, or decade browsing.

The home banner permanently features Journey — Departure (0125), Rippingtons — Live In LA (0741), and Tommy Emmanuel — Little by Little (0470), in that order, as chosen by Tom. These do not rotate or shuffle.

Preserve original covers. Generate web copies automatically at the actual display sizes. Initial local derivatives are 720px full images and 360px thumbnails; originals are not enlarged or cropped. Check real cover quality with Tom before publishing. Change the single cover base URL when permanent hosting is chosen. Original cover hosting, website hosting, and GitHub source control are separate decisions.

## Before release

The six personal decade introductions and closing passage were approved and implemented together, including the five playlist links, in `site/stories.mjs`. Writer surnames now appear beside tracks with exact, explicitly titled credit matches; ambiguous or conflicting credits remain accessible in the expandable full-credit section. Finish visual review, check grouping ambiguities, and choose hosting before configuring deployment. No ongoing manual curation should be necessary.
